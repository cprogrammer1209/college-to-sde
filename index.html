      If you follow this roadmap and prepare well, you can aim for the packages below. Each stage of learning moves you into a higher band of offers, and a better package recovers your college fees faster, in around 1 to 2 years instead of 4 to 5. Once the fees are recovered, your salary is yours to shape: support your family, build savings, invest early and keep growing.

      What the packages look like

      Fresher offers run from about ₹3 lakh to well over ₹30 lakh a year, and which band you land in depends on how well you prepare. Approximate 2026 CTC in lakhs per annum (LPA). Click a company to see how students reach it, and to find interview experiences for that track.

      Figures are approximate and come from public sources and candidate reports. Companies change them every hiring year, and many do not publish fresher pay. CTC is the total package, not take-home. Where a package includes stock, it vests over several years, so the first year pays less in cash.

      The band you land in is decided by preparation

      The gap between the lowest and highest bands at the same company comes down to test scores and coding ability.

      • TCSOne test, the NQT. Your score decides whether you get Ninja, Digital or Prime.
      • WiproTurbo uses the same exam as Elite, with a higher cutoff and an advanced coding round.
      • InfosysSpecialist Programmer and Digital Specialist Engineer roles have tougher coding rounds than System Engineer.
      • ZohoAptitude and coding rounds first, then technical and HR interviews.
